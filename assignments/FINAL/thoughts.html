Best way to approach a grilled cheese

This grilled cheese recipe is perfect for a late monday morning just before taking final exams. If you are on a rush this is the perfect sandwich for you.

7-minute grilled cheese

  • Prep time: 2 minutes
  • Cook time: 5 minutes
  • Total time: 7 minutes
  • Servings: 1 sandwich

Ingredients

  • 2 sliced sourdough bread
  • 1 tablespoon mayonaise
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 slice sharp Cheddar
  • 1 slice Monterey Jack
  • 4 slices pickled jalapeños, well drained
  • Equipment:non-stick pan & spatula

Instructions:

1.Prep the bread

Spread the mayonnaise evenly on one side of each slice of bread. This will be the outside of your sandwich. Turn the slices over so the mayo side is facing down on your cutting board.

2. Layer the flavor

Spread the Dijon mustard evenly across the dry inside of one slice of bread. Layer the slice of sharp Cheddar on top of the mustard, follow with the pickled jalapeño slices, and top with the Monterey Jack cheese. Cover with the second slice of bread, mayo side facing up.

3.Cook till golden

Place the sandwich into a cold skillet. Turn the heat to medium. Starting in a cold pan allows the cheese to melt fully by the time the bread toasts.

4.Flip and press

Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom is deeply golden brown. Flip the sandwich, press down firmly with your spatula to maximize crust contact, and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until the cheese is completely melted..

5. Serve

Remove from the heat, slice diagonally, and serve immediately while hot.
